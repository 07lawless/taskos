Getting started

What is Tasklist?

A calm place for your day: what to do today, how long it took, and which brand it was for.

Tasklist is built around one idea: do the most important thing first. Each day you pick a single task as your frog, start a timer when you begin, and the time lands on the right brand automatically. Around that sit a calendar, notes, bookmarks, brand kits, a short wellbeing check-in and a weekly view of where your hours went.

Your first five minutes

  1. Add a few tasks. Type into "New task for today" at the bottom of Today, or press the + button.
  2. Add your brands. In Settings, open Brands and add the clients or areas you work on. Give each one a colour.
  3. Pick your frog. Drag the task that matters most onto the frog box, or tap Choose.
  4. Start the timer when you begin and stop it when you're done. The time is logged against the task and its brand.
  5. Check your week on Friday to see where the hours went, and copy the report if you need it.

Nothing slips

If a task was due yesterday and you didn't finish it, it doesn't vanish. It stays on Today under From earlier days with a clear "From yesterday" or "2 days late". Use Move all to today or Tomorrow to reschedule the lot in one go.

Working with tasks

  • Click a task to see its details on the right. Double-click it to open the full task.
  • Right-click a task, or use its ⋯ button, for quick actions: reschedule, start a timer, make it the frog, move it to the bin.
  • Tick the circle to complete it. Untick it from Recently completed if you change your mind.

Suggest a date

Not sure when to fit something in? Open the Date dropdown and choose Suggest a date. Tasklist looks at the task's priority and estimate, and at how much is already due on each working day, then suggests a quiet day with a reason, like "Monday has room: 1 other task (1h 30m) already due." It skips weekends and works offline. Tap Use this date to accept it.

Today's frog and the timer

Your frog is the one task you do before anything else.

There's only ever one frog. To choose it, drag a task onto the frog box at the top of Today, tap Choose, or switch on Today's frog in the task's details. Picking a new frog replaces the old one.

Timing your work

Press Start on any task. A small pill appears at the top of the screen (above the bottom bar on a phone) showing the task and the time running, wherever you are in the app. Press Stop when you're done and the time is logged against the task and its brand.

Forgot to start the timer? Use Add time in the task's details to log 15 minutes, an hour or any amount, on any day.

Focus mode

For deep work, open Focus mode for a full-screen timer with breaks built in. It logs time the same way.

"Eat a live frog first thing in the morning and nothing worse will happen to you the rest of the day." The method comes from Brian Tracy's book Eat That Frog!

Repeating tasks

Set it once and Tasklist creates the next one for you.

In the task's details, open Repeat and choose daily, weekly or monthly. For weekly you can pick the days, like every other week on Monday and Wednesday. You can also choose when it ends: never, on a date, or after a number of times.

On a repeating task you can apply edits to upcoming, which copies the name, notes, priority, brand and estimate to every repeat still to come, or stop repeating. Stopping moves the upcoming repeats to the bin and keeps the current one.

Repeating tasks need an account.

Undo and the bin

Deleting something is never a disaster.

  • When you move a task to the bin, an Undo button appears for a few seconds.
  • Tasks, notes, bookmarks and brand kits stay in the Bin for 24 hours, each with a countdown and a Restore button. After that they're removed for good.
  • You can empty the bin early. Because that can't be undone, Tasklist asks you to confirm first.
  • Ctrl Z undoes your last change to a task, and Ctrl Y redoes it (⌘ on a Mac).
  • Guests get the same bin, kept on their device.

Planning

Calendar

Your month at a glance.

Every task with a date appears on its day. Drag a task to another day to reschedule it. Tap a day to see its tasks and the time you logged that day by brand, and use + Add to create a task straight onto that date. Overdue tasks are flagged so nothing slips by.

Brands

Brands are the clients or areas you work on, like Northwind, Bluebird Studio or Internal.

Add and colour them in Settings › Brands. Give a task a brand and its time adds up under that brand in your week, the share page and the CSV export. Each brand also gets its own list on the board.

If you bill different clients for your time, this is the part that saves you the most: at the end of the week the hours are already split, ready to copy.

Workspaces

Keep separate parts of your life apart.

Each workspace has its own tasks and brands, so Personal never mixes with Work. Switch from the top of the sidebar, or from More › Workspace on a phone. You can create as many as you like, each with its own name, emoji and colour. Notes and bookmarks are shared across every workspace.

Guests have one workspace. Signing in lets you add more.

Things to keep

Notes

Meeting notes, ideas and write-ups, in a simple dated list.

Notes are listed newest first and grouped by month, with each note's date on the left. Pin the ones you need often to keep them at the top, and search across every title and note.

The editor handles headings, bold, italic, lists, links, tables and pasted images. Each note has a date you can change, which is handy for writing up a meeting afterwards.

Bookmarks

Useful links, next to the work.

Paste a link and Tasklist fetches the page title, description and preview image for you. Star the important ones, mark things as read, and group them into collections such as Tools, Reading or Reference. You can edit any bookmark later.

Brand kits

Every brand's logo, colours, fonts and files in one place.

Brand kits open as a gallery. Each card shows the logo on the brand's main colour, its colours as a strip, and an "Aa" in its heading font. Open one to see the full kit:

Give a kit the same name as one of your task brands and its page also shows that brand's open tasks and hours this week, with a link to see them on the board.

For now: guidelines, the description and the website are saved on the device you add them on. The logo, colours, fonts and files sync everywhere. Brand kits need an account.

You

Wellbeing

A ten-second check-in on how you're doing.

Open Wellbeing whenever you like and rate your mood, energy, stress and sleep, with an optional note. There's no pop-up nagging you; it's there when you want it.

Your week shows in colour, and once there's enough to go on, Tasklist offers one useful insight, such as how your mood compares on days you finished your frog before lunch.

Your week

Where your time went, brand by brand.

  • Hours logged this week, compared with last week.
  • Tasks completed.
  • Hours by brand, as a simple bar for each.
  • Copy report puts a tidy weekly summary on your clipboard, ready to paste into an email or invoice.
  • Export CSV downloads any week's timesheet: date, brand, task, minutes and hours.

Your working hours (set in Settings) shape the week view.

Levels and rewards

A light, optional bit of fun for keeping going.

  • Finishing tasks earns XP. Your frog, steps and streaks earn a little more. There's a daily cap to keep it healthy.
  • XP raises your level, up to 50. Working on consecutive days builds a streak.
  • Levelling up unlocks accent themes: Sunset at 7, Ocean at 18, Royal at 30 and Inferno at 45.
  • At level 50 you can prestige: your level goes back to 1, but your total XP, streak and rewards are kept.
  • The scoreboard ranks people by prestige, level and XP, showing names and avatars only. Email addresses are never shown.

Levels and rewards need an account.

Sharing

Share links

One read-only link for your manager or client.

Create a link in Settings › Share links, for one workspace or all of them. Anyone with the link sees a clean page with Today, Upcoming this week and Overdue, plus tasks done and hours by brand for this week, last week or the month. It updates as you work, so there's no report to write.

Viewers can't change anything. Revoke the link in Settings and it stops working straight away. Share links need an account.

On your phone

Everything works on a phone, laid out for your thumb.

  • The bottom bar has Board, Calendar, Analytics and More. More holds Wellbeing, Notes, Bookmarks, Brands, Levels and rewards, your workspace and Settings.
  • Your lists become a row of pills you can swipe through. A task's details slide up from the bottom.
  • The + button adds a task from anywhere, and the timer pill sits just above the bottom bar.
  • Add Tasklist to your home screen from your browser's share menu and it opens like an app.

Your data and privacy

Your data is yours. It's never sold or shared for advertising.

When you sign in, your tasks, brands, notes, bookmarks and wellbeing check-ins are stored securely against your account. Google Sign-In is used only to sign you in. As a guest, the same things are kept in your browser on that device instead.

A few small preferences are always kept in your browser, like light or dark mode, whether the sidebar is collapsed, and which list you last had open on the board.

Questions

Is Tasklist free?+

Yes. Everything in these docs is free, with no subscription.

Do I need an account?+

No. Choose "Continue without an account" on the sign-in page. Your tasks stay on that device. Sign in with Google when you want them synced everywhere, and to use repeating tasks, brand kits, share links and levels.

Will my guest tasks move across when I sign in?+

Not at the moment. Signing in starts a fresh account, so it's best to sign in early if you plan to keep using Tasklist.

What happens to unfinished tasks at the end of the day?+

They stay on Today under "From earlier days", clearly marked as late, until you finish them or move them. Nothing disappears.

How do I get a timesheet for a client?+

Give your tasks a brand and time them as you work. In Your week, Copy report gives you a summary by brand, and Export CSV downloads the full timesheet.

What's the difference between Brands and Brand kits?+

Brands are the labels on your tasks, used to split your time. Brand kits hold a brand's logo, colours, fonts and files. Give a kit the same name as a brand and the two are linked.

Can I get back something I deleted?+

Yes, for 24 hours. Open the Bin and tap Restore. Right after deleting a task you can also tap Undo.

Does it work offline?+

As a guest, yes, since everything lives on your device. When signed in you'll need a connection to sync changes.

Where can I suggest a feature?+

Open What's new in the app to see every update and vote on the changes you'd like.
