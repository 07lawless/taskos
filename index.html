Today, at a glance.

Today, Upcoming, No date and Overdue sit side by side with the brands you work for.

Focus

Eat the frog first.

Pick the one task that matters most and do it before anything else. Start the timer when you begin, stop it when you're done, and the time is logged.

✓Steps keep big jobs moving. Tick them off as you go.
◉Always visible. A small pill at the top shows the timer running wherever you are.
Track

See where your hours go.

Every minute you log lands on the right brand. Your week shows the split, tasks done and a report you can copy in one tap.

⏱Timer or by hand. Add 15 minutes, an hour or any amount for any day.
⇩Timesheet export. Download any week as a CSV.
Share

One link for your manager.

A read-only page with what you're working on, what's next and what's done, with hours by brand for this week, last week or the month.

🔗Always up to date. It updates as you work, so there's no report to write.
✕Turn it off any time. Revoke the link from Settings and it stops working at once.
